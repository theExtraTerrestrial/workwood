Round 19: where the Windows / WSL switch lives

It is rarely used, so it should not sit at the top of the sidebar.

Today it is a two-button switch next to the "Sessions" title. Each option below moves it somewhere quieter. Menus are drawn open so you can see them.

AIn the gear menu

The switch becomes one row in the gear menu at the bottom. Out of the way; two clicks to change. The gear carries the dot when the other side needs you. Recommended.
