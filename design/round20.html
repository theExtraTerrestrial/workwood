To decide with it

  • When it goes compact. My suggestion: by itself when the window is narrower than about 1000 pixels, and also whenever you press the sidebar button (or Ctrl+B) at any width. Your choice is remembered; widening the window brings the full sidebar back only if you had not collapsed it yourself.
  • With a side panel open (a preview, changes, the comparison) the stage is squeezed from both sides. My suggestion: the sidebar also goes compact by itself whenever a side panel leaves the conversation less than about 480 pixels.
