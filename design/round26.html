Round 26: where accounts live · wireframes

Accounts

These are layout sketches only; the look comes after we pick one. In every one, the message box line stays the main meter: the account this session runs on, with its 5h and 7d bars. Every popover uses the same one-line row per account (bottom of the page). Switch between 3, 6 and 12 accounts at the top. Drag a window's right edge to see it narrow; below about 600px the sidebar turns into a strip. Click the dashed spots.

AMessage box only

Nothing new in the sidebar. The account name under the message box opens the list. When any account gets close to or reaches a limit, a dot appears on the name.

Takes no room. Already where your eyes areYou only see other accounts when you look

BOne line at the foot of the sidebar my pick

The foot shows one account: the one closest to a limit, or the next to clear when one is full. It also says how many others there are. Click it to open the list, upward. The line stays one line however many accounts there are.

The account to worry about is always in view, in fixed spaceOnly one is named; the rest are a count

CA row of small gauges at the foot

Each account is a narrow vertical gauge, filled as far as it is used. The account of the open session is outlined. Hover one to see its name; click to open the list. Twelve fit in about 120px; more wrap onto a second row at most.

You see everyone's level at a glance, in fixed spaceNo names until you hover. Hard to read past ~15

DAn “Accounts” tab next to Sessions

The sidebar head gets two tabs. Accounts swaps the session list for the account list, so it gets the full height when you want it and none when you don't. The tab shows a dot when any account is near a limit.

Room for many accounts, and for more detail per row laterHides the sessions while you look

EIn the conversation header

A compact summary at the right of the header (“5 with room · 1 full”) that opens the list. The sidebar is untouched.

Visible from any session, takes no sidebar roomThe header is already busy (worktree, changes, panels)

The row, shared by every option

One line per account: name, one bar for the limit closest to full (the mark shows how far its window has run), the %, and when it resets. When there's no room, the reset time goes. More than about 7 accounts and the list scrolls; the ones closest to a limit come first.

300px wide: everything.
220px wide: the reset time goes.
