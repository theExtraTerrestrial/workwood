Round 27 · the account menu

Accounts

The menu under the message box, four ways. Drag each frame's right edge to narrow it. The width shows in the corner.

What's wrong now

  • It's a fixed 360px wide, so below about 450px it runs off the window.
  • Name and pills on the left, two meter rows on the right: the columns don't line up, and pills crowd the name.
  • "5h / 7d" and the reset times repeat on every row, so it reads as a wall of small text.
  • The line under the box doesn't wrap: at narrow widths the mode and model sit on top of the meters.
  • The red dot sits on live even when it's full that's out of room.

AOne line per account

Shows only the limit closest to full (labelled 5h or 7d), with its bar, % and reset time. Narrower: the bar goes first, then the reset time.

BA small table my pick

5h and 7d are labelled once at the top. Each cell shows the %, its reset time and a bar. Narrower: just the two numbers.

CTwo-line cards

Name and status on top, then 5h and 7d side by side. Roomiest, but the tallest: 12 accounts means scrolling.

DA short list, and detail for the one you're on

Each row is a dot and the worst %. The account this session runs on gets a card with two big numbers.

Under the box, in every option

The line wraps instead of overlapping. Below about 430px only the 5h meter stays. The dot on the account name means this account is near its limit. If another account is the one that's full, it shows only in the menu.
