How it loads

  • Known now, drawn now. Session titles, project tiles and profile names come with the window; only what needs a read waits.
  • Same box, before and after. A skeleton is the size of what replaces it, so nothing below it moves. One shimmer runs across the whole page, not one per block.
  • Last time’s shape. How many rows, cards and pictures each section had is remembered, so the skeleton guesses right. A section that turns out empty keeps one quiet line (“All committed”) instead of vanishing.
  • Never a skeleton twice. After the first load, readings update in place.

Under the hood
  • /home and /home/activity already arrive separately; each section waits only for its own.
  • activity() reads 28 days of turns and 8 days of minutes. Time needs a range: /home/activity?from=&to=, minutes kept as hour buckets per project per day so a year stays small.
  • Transcripts go after cleanupPeriodDays (30 by default). A year needs ~/.workwood/time.jsonl: one line per project per day, written as scan() reads.
