All 33, and how they change
    Under the hood
    • One attribute set: data-tip is the name, data-tip-d is the dim line, data-tip-k holds the keys. Today's “(Ctrl+K)” suffixes move into data-tip-k. data-tip-full means: show the element's own text when it's clipped (scrollWidth > clientWidth); for paths, the folder is dimmed.
    • showTip() in ui/app.js builds the box from those, measures it, centres it, then clamps it to the window. The fixed width:300px on .tip2 becomes a max-width.
    • The 4 places that set .title in code (Full width, Jump, Send/Stop, limit note) set dataset.tip instead. The 3 <iframe title> stay: they name the frame for screen readers and show no tooltip.
    • A test fails on any title= or .title= left in ui/ outside an iframe, so this doesn't creep back.
    • Settings: drop the .dlg:has(.pfx) resize and the dead 680 × 460 rule; .dlg becomes 760×540 with max-height:92vh.
