What changes

  • Look replaces the Home tab. Theme moves here from General as Dark · Light · Auto; Auto follows the system and switches live.
  • Background for Home · Sessions · Sidebar, each with its own pick, Motion and Warn at limits.
  • Motion: Always · While working · Never. "While working" moves only while an agent works — that session's agent in a session, any agent on Home and in the sidebar — and slows to rest when the turn ends. "Always" also speeds up while one works.
  • Warn at limits is separate: warm near a limit, red at it, even when still or None.
  • The sidebar takes the inbox's colours like Home, sits under a light veil so names stay readable, and its limit glow rises from the bottom. Constellation stays Home-only.
  • Defaults: Home as today (Aurora, Always, warn on). Sessions: None, While working, warn on. Sidebar: None, While working, warn off — so a limit doesn't glow in two places at once.
  • Calm, for reading: Paper and Lines never move; Dusk and Edges drift slowly. In a session, moving kinds dim and keep out of the reading column.
Under the hood
  • Local settings like today: ww.bg.home|session|side, ww.motion.* (always|working|never), ww.limits.*. ww.hbg carries over to Home; ww.hlive off becomes Motion Always + warn off.
  • ww.theme gains auto: the page follows prefers-color-scheme and listens for changes; the terminal theme follows too.
  • Two layers: one behind .main (Home or the open session), one behind the sidebar's list. Each paints once per kind and changes only classes and playback after that.
  • Coming to rest: running animations ramp playbackRate to 0 over ~2 s, and back up when work starts. Reduced-motion and a hidden window still stop everything.
