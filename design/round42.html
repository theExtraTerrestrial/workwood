Auto picks by how much went on, not length: Haiku for short, mechanical sessions, Sonnet for most, Opus when there were many messages from you, failed commands or reversed decisions. The chevron on any Hand off overrides it once.

What changed

  • Always there, easy to find: in the context menu, as /handoff next to /compact when you type /, and in a strip above the message box from 400k tokens, or ¾ of the window if it is smaller (150k on a 200k model): how sharp earlier turns stay depends on how much there is, not on what share of the window it fills. It is an offer, not a warning; the figure still turns amber at 80%.
  • No review step: Hand off starts at once. The seam shows the handoff being written, then folds it so you can open it later. The model and effort come from Settings; the chevron changes them for one handoff.
  • The handoff adds “Tried”: what failed and why, so the next session doesn't repeat it. A larger model writes this part better on long threads.
  • Browse, in the turn browser you have: a session without a thread keeps today's flat list. Once there is a thread, it becomes “Thread · 12 sessions”, its turns grouped by session under a one-line topic, the current one open. Find searches the whole thread; matching sessions open with their hits.
  • 10+ sessions: the thread holds the current session and the one before; the rest wait behind one line (“Sessions 1–10 · Browse”), and scrolling up loads one more at a time. The rail shows a dot where each session starts and “+10” for those not loaded. Past four, the sidebar and header show a count instead of dots.
  • Jump back: picking an old turn shows its session with the rest folded on both sides, and “Back to now” returns.
  • Looking back is the agent's: it searches the earlier sessions when it is about to retry something or isn't sure what was decided. You just ask in plain words; there is nothing to learn.
  • Context menu: one big number, a bar, three words of legend, two actions on one line each.
Under the hood
  • Writer, in two parts: without a model, the service pulls from the .jsonl every message you wrote and your answers on question cards (word for word), the files changed, and the failed commands with their errors. The model gets the history stripped of bulky tool output and writes only the judgement: where it is, decided, tried and why it failed, open. It runs as claude -p --model … --effort ….
  • New session: started with the handoff in --append-system-prompt (it survives later compactions) and an MCP server with one tool, recall(query), whose description tells the agent when to use it.
  • Search scope costs nothing in context: recall returns at most ~5 short quoted excerpts, about 1.5k tokens, whether it searches one session or ten. So it searches the whole chain, newest first.
  • Chain: {prev, next} per session in the service's own metadata. The thread loads earlier sessions when you scroll up or pick one in the outline. Claude Code's own files stay untouched.
  • /handoff is Workwood's own command; the service handles it instead of sending it to Claude Code.

Decided

  • The strip stays an offer; nothing hands off by itself.
  • The menu says what each choice does; Handoff and Compact are only named underneath.
