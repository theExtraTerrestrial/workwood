Read aloud

round 50 · Bella, on the processor
Where the player goes is a setting: above the box by default, under the answer if you pick it, and under the answer anyway when another strip already holds the box. A speaker beside an answer's Copy reads that turn's answers. While it reads, the sentence being read is lit, and a player lets you pause, go back or forward a sentence, and change speed. Click any sentence to read from there.

Same in both

  1. Code blocks are skipped with “code block”; tables are read row by row; links read their words.
  2. Esc stops; with the message box empty, Space pauses and ← → step a sentence.
  3. Back at the first sentence of an answer goes to the end of the answer before it, in the same turn.
  4. Sending a message doesn't stop it; starting another answer's speaker does.

When another strip is up

Only one strip sits on the box. Whichever was there first keeps it.

A long answer

Past 12 sentences the line stops being segments: it fills smoothly, with a notch where each paragraph starts. Click the line to jump; Shift with ← → steps a paragraph.

In the preview

A page in the preview reads aloud too, three ways: the speaker in its header reads the whole page; pointing at a heading shows Read section, which reads up to the next heading of its level; selecting text shows Read aloud for just that. The page itself is never changed: the lit sentence is drawn over it.

Under the hood
  • The service keeps one Kokoro worker (~/.local/share/kokoro-tts, CPU, voice af_bella) and starts it on the first press: about 1 s, then about 0.8 GB of RAM until it has been idle for 2 minutes.
  • Text is split into sentences; each becomes its own short clip, made about 5× faster than it plays, so the first one plays within about half a second and the rest are ready before they're needed.
  • The window keeps the clips it has played, so pause, back and forward are instant and make nothing again; speed is the player's playback rate (pitch kept), not a new clip.
  • Nothing leaves the machine. Without the Kokoro folder the speaker doesn't show.
