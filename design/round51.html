Stacked strips, a mini player, what a preview reads

Wireframes, round 51. Settled: reading aloud happens only in the session you're looking at; switch away and it pauses.

1 · Two strips at once

The cache countdown and the context offer can both want the box today. Three ways to stack them; the newest or most urgent is on top.

2 · The reading mini player

Not a strip: a small pill that floats over the conversation. Drag it by its grip; it snaps to the nearest spot and stays there next time.

3 · What a preview reads

Most previews are layout. Only sections of words get a speaker: open questions, notes, what changed. Frames and pictures never do.

  1. The agent marks them. Design rounds are asked to put notes and questions in <section data-read>. Marked sections always get a speaker.
  2. Otherwise Workwood guesses: a heading whose section is mostly words (lists, paragraphs, at least about 20 words, no frames or pictures) gets one; anything named like Open questions, Questions or Notes always does.
  3. The header's speaker reads every such section in order, open questions first. On a page with none, it doesn't show.
