A card deck of strips, a dockable mini player

Wireframes, round 52. Settled: the preview's speaker reads open questions first, then the other sections of words.

1 · Strips become cards

Each strip is a card that floats just above the box, with rounded corners and a shadow. When there's more than one, the others stack behind it and the newest or most urgent is on top.

  1. Point at the deck or click its count and the cards fan out upward. Move away and they fold back.
  2. Closing the top card brings the next one forward.
  3. One card on its own looks the same, just without cards behind it.

2 · The mini player docks

It starts at bottom center, on top of the deck. Drag its grip and six spots appear: top and bottom of the conversation, each left, center or right. Drop it on one and it stays there next time.

Questions

  1. Fanned deck: should it open on pointing (hover), or only on a click of the count?
  2. Bottom spots: should the player always sit above the deck, rising as cards stack, or overlap the conversation at a fixed height?
