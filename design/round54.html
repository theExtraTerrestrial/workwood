Strips stay docked to the box. When several want it, the newest or most urgent is in front and the others show as lips behind it. A small tab at the top right counts them and opens Show all. The read-aloud player can be A a strip, B under the answer, or C a mini player you can move.

The deck

Player placement

A setting with three options. In A the player is always in front of the deck. In B and C the strips stay as they are.

Moving the mini player

Drag its grip and two segmented rows appear, one at the top of the conversation and one at the bottom: left · center · right. The segment under the pointer lights. Drop it and it stays there, in new sessions too.
