Under the hood
  • Kokoro moves from Python to Node: kokoro-js on the transformers.js Workwood already has, the same Kokoro-82M model and voices.
  • Downloaded from Hugging Face (onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX) into ~/.cache/workwood/models, like Whisper. No Python, no venv, no sudo.
  • ~/.local/share/kokoro-tts and speech-worker.py are no longer needed.
  • The driver line shows on WSL only, where the card sits behind the Windows driver. On Linux or macOS without a card, the row stays hidden.
