Under the hood
  • Home on the phone is the desktop Home (Needs you, Left off, Profiles) laid out in one column; the approval card uses the same answer path as the session's.
  • Notifications are Web Push from the service, sent through the phone browser's push service; the phone's key holds its subscription. Unpair or Remove drops both.
  • Setup steps are checked by asking the Tailscale CLI for its status (installed, signed in, peers, HTTPS cert). Step 3 ticks when a second device appears.
