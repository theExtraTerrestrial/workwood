3 · Stars you can read and answer

Each star is a session. Hover one for what it's doing. A star that needs you turns amber, breathes slowly and says why; click it to answer there.

  • Working: the ring and the light along the figure, as now. Needs you: amber and a slow breath, with a short label. Just finished: the flare and shooting star, then a white afterglow for a few minutes.
  • Hover shows the step and last line; click answers if it's waiting, otherwise opens the session.
  • When you're not looking: the window title counts what needs you, (1) Workwood, with an optional soft chime.
Under the hood
  • Nothing new from the service: each session's state, current step, last line and waiting question already reach the sidebar.
  • The Wall is a Home view like the others, remembered with them; Look's Wall switch hides it.
  • The sky already knows working stars (skyMark); amber and afterglow are two more classes on the same stars, so the sky never redraws.
  • Phone: no dock, no Wall, no star cards; Home's Pick up already leads with what waits.
