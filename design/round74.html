Quiet should look on purpose: the time, the day, one line that says all is well, and the profiles' limits living in the scene itself. When something starts, the clock goes and the dock rises; it comes back once all is quiet.

Constellation: a moon per profile

Its phase is what is left of the 5-hour limit: full when fresh, a crescent when low, dark with a thin rim at the limit, with the time it comes back. The faint arc around it is the week.
