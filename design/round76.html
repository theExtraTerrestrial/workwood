Round 76 · at the PC, or away

While you're at the PC, the phone stays quiet. A notification waits a minute, and if you handle it here it never goes out. When the PC has had no input for a while, or is locked or asleep, you're away, and the phone gets everything as it does now. One small mark says which it is.

Where the mark sits

It should be in sight but take no room from the sessions.

A · Sidebar footerRecommended next to Done and Settings. Always in sight, even in a session.

B · Top bar beside the view switch or the session's buttons. It moves between Home and a session.

C · Under the message box with the limits meter. It's hidden on Home's Wall and when the compose box is closed.

The mark, in its three states

It appears only when a phone has notifications on. Click it to switch by hand. The countdown is the only thing that moves.

Under the hood
  • Idle comes from Electron's powerMonitor.getSystemIdleTime(), which counts input to the whole computer, not just Workwood, plus its lock, suspend and resume events. In WSL the Windows window reads it and passes it to the service; on Linux and macOS the window reads it directly.
  • With only the browser page (no window), activity on the page counts instead.
  • The service holds each push for the chosen time and drops it when the session is answered, opened or the turn is read on the PC (the looking check already used for phones, extended to the PC).
  • "Away now" by hand lasts until the next input after a minute has passed. "I'm here" lasts until you go idle again.
  • Limit pushes (near, cleared) follow the same rule; held ones are sent when the hold ends unless something on the PC showed them.
